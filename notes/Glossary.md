This file contains linguistic terminology used and special notations.

Notations:
- V: verb
- A, いA: i-adjective
- なA: na-adjective
- N: noun or na-adjective used as noun
- ~: generic placeholder for full (relative) sentences

- 用言: inflectable words
	- 動詞: verb
	- 形容詞: i-adjective, proper adjective
	- 形容動詞: na-adjective, nominal adjective
- 無用言: non-inflecting words
	- 体言: substantives
		- 名詞: noun
		- 代名詞: pronoun
	- 副詞: adverbs
	- 接続詞: conjunctions
	- 感動詞: interjections
	- 連体詞: adnominal adjectives
- 付属語: dependent words
	- 助動詞: auxiliaries
	- 助詞: particles
- 極性: polarity
	- 肯定: affirmative
	- 否定: negative
