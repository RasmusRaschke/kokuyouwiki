# 助数詞 + くらい

- 助数詞 + くらい・ぐらい
- Same as [[ほど]]

# くらい

- V連体形・Aい・N + (に・へ・で・と・から) + くらい・ぐらい + (が・を・に・へ・で・と・から)
- Expresses the extend to which something is approximately equal to or the approximate degree of something.
- Opposed to [[ほど]], it does not emphasise the preceding as an upper bound for the degree of the following, instead stating somewhat factually that the following may be more or less to the degree of the preceding. Depending on context, くらい may even be understood as "at least".
