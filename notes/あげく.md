# あげく（に）

- V音便形た + あげく(に)・あげくの〜・あげくの果には
- Expresses that an extended period of time was spend to reach some mostly undesirable or difficult result.
- Very similar to [[すえ]], but the agent must be human.
- The expression あげくの果には is extremely empathetic and reserved for significantly worrying outcomes.
